Creative Ops & Performance Marketing Agency for D2C, B2B, F&B and Healthcare Brands

Hyphening Media is a Bengaluru-based creative operations and marketing agency that helps D2C, B2B, F&B and healthcare brands scale through content strategy, video production, social media operations, web and app development, campaign management, and performance analytics. Talk to our team about your brand's goals.

Capabilities

  • Content Strategy: monthly plans with scripts, hooks and posting schedules
  • Video Production: short-form and long-form video from script to final edit
  • Social Media Ops: daily management across Instagram, YouTube and LinkedIn
  • Web Development: custom websites and web apps
  • App Development: mobile apps
  • Performance Analytics: a secure client portal with near real-time, platform-level performance
  • Campaign Management: organic and paid campaigns measured against enquiries, bookings and cost per acquisition
  • Brand Development: visual identity systems, positioning and tone-of-voice guides
  • News Media Coverage & PR: news-media placements that build authority

Our story

Hyphening Media scripts, shoots, produces and measures the work itself, so a campaign and its numbers sit under one roof. Reports lead with click-through rate, conversions and organic reach rather than vanity metrics. The agency is based in Bengaluru and led by Joseph George (CEO) and Deepanjan Sarkar (COO). Hyphening Media is spelled with "Hyphening" and is sometimes written as Hypening Media or Hyphen Media.
